START WITH THE END IN MIND

Before we decide what to do, we get clear on what winning looks like.

My philosophy is simple: start with why, define the end state, then work backwards into the few actions that will actually move the business.

I borrow heavily from the discipline of 4DX: focus on the wildly important, measure the lead actions, keep a visible scoreboard and create a cadence of accountability.

For an SME, that matters. You don't have endless layers, unlimited capital or time for strategy theatre. The plan has to be commercial, practical and executable by the people already in the business.

The objective isn't to make the business busier. It's to make the business better.
